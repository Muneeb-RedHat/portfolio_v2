01 / ABOUT

01

CURIOUS ABOUT HOW THINGS WORK.

I'm a Computer Engineering student at GIKI, and right now I'm building my technical foundations — one program, one circuit idea and one small website at a time.

I like knowing why something works, not just that it does. That curiosity pulls me toward the mechanisms underneath technology: how a line of code becomes a signal, and how a signal becomes motion.

What I learn only counts once it turns into something practical, so I'm aiming to turn it into real engineering projects, starting small and getting more ambitious. The long-term goal: increasingly capable autonomous robots, and one day a JARVIS-like AI assistant.

I'm interested in the layer where code meets circuits, where algorithms meet physical systems, and where ideas become things that actually work.

— MUNEEB SAJID
